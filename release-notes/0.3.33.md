# WilyTrader 0.3.33

- Makes Axiom Pulse quick-buy targets visible with yellow debug borders and persistent per-token labels.
- Keeps Pulse hit targets aligned through column scrolling, and hides targets when the ticker row content is covered by sticky headers.
- Makes Pulse auto-buy execution retry through Axiom SPA navigation and price hydration until a paper buy records or a terminal balance failure occurs.
- Replays Axiom chart entry lines and execution flags after chart rebinding or symbol changes.
