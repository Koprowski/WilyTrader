# WilyTrader 0.3.20

- Narrows right-click exit-order placement to the WilyTrader `100%` sell button.
- Shows two hover submenus: `Target Exit MC` for market caps above the current market cap and `Target Stop Loss MC` for market caps below it.
- Provides selectable market-cap levels in `$1K` increments and places the selected 100% target/stop order immediately.
- Locks WilyTrader target/stop chart lines to avoid the jumpy TradingView drag behavior seen in Axiom.
